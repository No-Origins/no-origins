Why it works

A pill with two eyes. The two o's in "no origins" become a face, and the face becomes a character — one per colour, one per block or agent. It's approachable at the maximum, it's curious by construction (it's looking), and it scales from a favicon to a full canvas of them talking to you. It also fits your next block exactly: an agents harness where every agent is a blob. Filled with colour it's a character; outlined in ink it's the logotype.
