The accents are lime and violet (2026-09-27). `--primary` is `--lime` with dark ink, and `--secondary` is `--violet`
with white, where both were shadcn's neutral greys. The grey of hover and of being active (`--muted`) is lime at 14%
mixed over the page, and a menu's highlighted item (`--accent`) is lime at 4% over the popover. Default buttons, the
pager's current page, progress bars, checkboxes, switches and sliders are lime, and secondary buttons and bubbles are
violet. `text-primary` (the Button's `link` variant, and the link hovers in Field, Item and Empty) is lime text, which is
about 1.3 : 1 on the light theme's white.
