The agent may have no tail (Orbit.md C23).

- `lib/agent-body`: the tail declares `tail`, a switch, default on, a look. A switch is read as its token is
  written, the word `on` or `off`.
- `lib/sphere-motion`: `SphereMotion.tail`, `SPHERE_START.tail` on. Off, or at a Length of 0, `sphereFrame` draws
  nothing of a tail, sitting or flying. Until now a Length of 0 put every piece of the tail on the head at z −0, which
  read as in front of the page, and drew a disc 0.8 of the head across behind a shape.
