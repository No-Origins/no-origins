`GridPages` turns on ↑ ↓ as well as ← → (Grid.md D42; 2026-09-26). The ↓ key turns forward and ↑ back, the same as
the pager's arrow that shows that glyph. A key that a focused component has already handled, or one held with a
modifier, is left alone, and a key that turns the page no longer scrolls.
