`lib/sphere-motion.ts`, the sphere (Motion.md M17, 2026-09-30, his: "an energetic and calm, 3D sphere that travels by
diving from one cell to another"): a character for the motion studio's bench, now version 10 — his fish, a head and a
rubbery tail, jumping "from nest to nest", seen from the side, settling into the bottom of each nest's circle as into a
bowl (ball, jelly or slime) with its tail behind the page. Pure: `readSphereMotion` reads its 30
`--motion-sphere-*` tokens (none in globals.css yet; `SPHERE_START` is version 10's values); `sphereCourse` is the head
through a jump — sitting in its nest, a crouch, an arc to the nest `sphereJump` puts it, a landing that is a slippery ball in a
bowl (touching down on the side it comes from, sliding through the bottom and up the far side into a soft wall it
squishes against, coming back as lively and as soon as its energy and its come-back say, its jelly swaying), the nest giving — and `sphereStill` one that sits; `sphereSpine` is the tail, a spring chain
stepped at a fixed rate from the jump's start; `sphereFrame` draws it in flat colours only. Nothing in the package plays
it yet; the studio's stage paints it.
