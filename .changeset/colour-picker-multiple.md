The colour picker takes several picks (World.md W91; his, of a grove's trees: "allow me to select multi-colors so that
we can randomly distribute these colors on the trees that are placed").

- `components/colour-picker`: `multiple` makes it a pick of several, its `value` the picked ones in the options' order;
  pressing a picked swatch unpicks it, and the last one picked stays, so one is always picked. Without it, it picks one
  as before. The showcase's specimen shows both.
