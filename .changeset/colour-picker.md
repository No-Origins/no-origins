`ColourPicker` joins the design system, written for it since no registry has one (his ask, Orbit.md C17):
every pick of a colour, as swatches — Orbit's Paint line, moved into the package. It is composed from
`ToggleGroup`, one swatch a colour, the one picked pressed, each a circle (Grid.md D39) with a dot of its colour and
named in its title, standing together at the small size and sharing out a column too narrow for them rather than
wrapping.
